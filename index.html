Controls

MOUSE
the jet flies to your cursor · arrows nudge it
WSAD
throttle up / down · rudder: skid the nose left / right without banking, and the cannon aims where the nose points
SPACE
afterburner
RIGHT CLICKQE
tap: aileron roll the way the mouse moves, a sideways snap with brief invulnerability · hold: barrel roll, the jet corkscrews for as long as you hold it
SHIFT
airbrake · bleed speed, tighten the turn
SHIFT+PULL UP
reversal · chop the throttle and circle back; a bandit on your six flies straight past (or SHIFT + right click, or C)
V
cobra · nose past the vertical, the airframe becomes an airbrake, everything behind you overshoots
SHIFT+PUSH DOWNZ
split-S · roll inverted and pull through a downward loop: trade height for a fast, low exit
B
immelmann · half loop up and roll out on top: a fast climb that costs airspeed
CLICKR
intercept: cannon · missile when locked (also middle click) · boost to close, brake to let them by
